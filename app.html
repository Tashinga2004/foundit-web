Market Position

Where the other apps leave a gap

Four kinds of apps already exist in this space. Each is strong at one thing and thin somewhere else — that gap is where Foundit sits.

Troov — dedicated platform

Strong matching algorithm and institutional partners, but usefulness depends on a corporate partner network that takes time to build.

Gap: partner-dependent
Nextdoor — neighbourhood network

Verified neighbours and a large existing user base, but a lost item post competes with general chatter and quickly disappears down the feed.

Gap: buried in a general feed
PawBoost — pets only

Excellent viral reach through one-tap social sharing, but the entire model is built around pets and doesn't generalise to other lost items.

Gap: single category
Generic lost & found apps

Easy to use with a low barrier to entry, but weak on verification — claims are accepted with little proof, which invites false claims.

Gap: no ownership verification
